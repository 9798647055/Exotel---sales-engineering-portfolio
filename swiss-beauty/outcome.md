# Outcome and Lessons Learned

## Status
Implemented.

## Business value (expected)
- Customers get **order and delivery status over a call**, with no app or website needed
- Customers who need more help **reach the support team**
- Support agents **work on laptops**, with no mobile phones needed
- Change requests (delivery address, product) are handled on the same call
