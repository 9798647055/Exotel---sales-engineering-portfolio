# Sales Playbook: Order-Status IVR with WebRTC Agent Calling

Reusable notes for D2C brands, e-commerce and retail businesses that want automation with a human fallback.

## Elevator pitch
"Most order calls are 'where is my order'. An IVR on one number answers those automatically. When a customer needs more help, the call goes to your support agent right in the browser on their laptop. No mobile phones, no desk phones, no hardware."

## Ideal customer
- D2C brands and online retailers with steady order-status calls
- Support teams working on laptops, in an office or remotely
- Businesses that want to reduce agent time on routine queries but keep a human option

## Discovery questions
1. How do customers check order status today?
2. What share of support calls is about order status?
3. When a customer is unhappy, how do they reach your team?
4. Does your team use mobile phones, desk phones or laptops for calls?
5. How many agents, and what are their working hours?
6. What changes do customers ask for most (address, product, cancellation)?
7. Do agents work from the office, from home, or both?
8. Do you need call recording or quality monitoring?
9. Which languages do customers speak?

## Value proposition
| Client pain | Our answer |
|---|---|
| Too many routine status calls reach agents | IVR answers them automatically |
| Customers cannot reach a person when unhappy | Handover from the IVR to a support agent |
| Agents do not want to use mobile phones | WebRTC calling on laptops |
| Hardware and phone costs | Cloud-based, no desk phones needed |
| Customers need changes (address, product) | Agents take the request on the same call |

## Likely objections
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Will voice quality be good on a laptop?" | Quality depends on internet and headset. Set a simple readiness checklist and test before go-live. |
| "Our agents work from different places." | Browser-based calling works wherever agents have internet. |
| "Can we start small?" | Yes. Start with a small group of agents and add more later. |
| "Do customers have to install anything?" | No. Customers just call a number. |

## Cross-sell ideas
- Call recording and quality monitoring
- Call queues and routing across agents
- Callback requests when no agent is free
- WhatsApp or SMS order updates
- Feedback calls after delivery
