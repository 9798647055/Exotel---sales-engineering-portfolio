# Sales Playbook: Telematics Driver Alerts

Reusable notes for similar opportunities in transport, logistics and fleet businesses.

## Elevator pitch
"You already know when a bus is overspeeding or overheating. The gap is telling the driver at that moment. We connect your telematics platform to cloud telephony, so every critical event triggers an automatic call to the right driver, in real time and with no manual monitoring."

## Ideal customer
- Bus, cab, logistics or fleet operators
- Already use a telematics / GPS platform
- Care about driver safety, compliance and incident reduction

## Discovery questions
1. How do you currently alert a driver when a safety event occurs?
2. How long does it take from the event to the driver knowing?
3. Who monitors the telematics dashboard, and during what hours?
4. Which events matter most: speed, engine, driving hours, fatigue?
5. Does your telematics platform support sending events to an external API?
6. Do you have bus-to-driver mapping with contact numbers?
7. What languages do your drivers need alerts in?
8. How many vehicles and expected alerts per day?

## Value proposition
| Client pain | Our answer |
|---|---|
| Manual monitoring is slow and needs people | Fully automated alerts |
| Driver is reached late | Immediate outbound voice call |
| Fear of replacing the telematics system | Works with the existing platform through an API |
| Driver cannot look at a screen | Voice call needs no screen or app |

## Likely objections
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Our telematics vendor may not support this." | Only an event or webhook needs to be sent, which most platforms can do. Confirm with their tech team early. |
| "Drivers may not pick up while driving." | Alert design (retries, short messages) is part of the workflow. Confirm the handling approach with the client. |
| "Will it need new hardware?" | No. It is cloud-based. |
| "Can we add more alert types later?" | Yes. Each new signal is a new event and message mapping. |

## Cross-sell ideas
- Alerts to supervisors or fleet managers
- SMS or WhatsApp follow-ups
- Passenger notifications for delays
- IVR for drivers to report issues
