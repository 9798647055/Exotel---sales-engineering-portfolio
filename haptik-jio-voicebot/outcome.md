# Outcome and Lessons Learned

## Status
Implemented.

## Business value (expected)
- Recharge reminders **automated** across a very large customer base
- **Repeated attempts** improve the chance of reaching the customer
- **Two-way conversation**, including offer and discount queries
- **Lower cost and latency** than a streaming-channel approach
- **Chat-to-call** continuity through the outbound call API
